ASSIGNMENT 

Construct an EPC diagram for the process described below. The diagram shall include functions, events, and organizational units.

Galando is a company that produces and sells tablets. The package used to deliver a Galando tablet includes three components:

• a Galando tablet

• a charger

• a headset

Before a package is sent to a customer, its contents need to be inspected. This is done in the following way. The Galando tablet, the charger, and the headset are inspected in parallel. When all have been inspected, they are put into a plastic box, which is delivered to the customer. The Galando tablet is first inspected by a junior inspector for visible hardware damages, such as cracks of the screen. If any visible damage is detected, the tablet is sent to a senior inspector who will decide how to proceed. She may either repair the damage immediately and return the tablet to the junior inspector or decide to abort the inspection process. In the latter case, the entire process stops. If and when the tablet is returned to the junior inspector, he will inspect the software installation. He will select three applications at random and check if these work. If one application does not work, he will reinstall it until it works. However, he will not try to reinstall it more than five times. 

When all three applications are inspected and work correctly, the inspection of the Galando tablet is completed. As stated above, the charger and the headset are inspected in parallel with the Galando tablet. The charger is inspected and if it does not work, it is discarded (thrown away) and a new charger is inspected instead. This will be repeated until a working charger is found. The headset is inspected and if it does not work, it is discarded and another headset is added to the package. Note that this new headset is not inspected.
