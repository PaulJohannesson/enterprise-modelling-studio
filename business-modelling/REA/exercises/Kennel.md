# REA Exercise: Kennel and Puppy Sales

## Scenario

Consider a kennel that sells puppies to its customers. A customer can order a puppy in either of two ways.

### Case 1: Ordering a Specific Puppy

The customer wants a particular puppy that they have seen during a visit to the kennel. When placing the order, the customer specifies the puppy by providing its name or identification number.

### Case 2: Ordering by Breed and Gender

The customer wants a puppy of a particular breed and gender but does not select a specific puppy when placing the order. The customer therefore specifies only the desired breed and gender.

## Delivery Options

In both cases, the customer can choose between two forms of delivery:

- **Collection at the kennel:** The customer visits the kennel and collects the puppy.
- **Home delivery:** The kennel delivers the puppy to the customer’s home.

## Information Requirements

Your solutions to Tasks A and B must support answers to the following questions:

1. Which customers have ordered a poodle puppy? *(Poodle is a breed.)*
2. How many customers ordered more than one puppy during 2015?
3. Which customers have ordered a puppy that has not yet been delivered?
4. Which customers have ordered puppies of different breeds?
5. Which customers have ordered female puppies?

## Tasks

### A. Exchange Process for a Specific Puppy

Construct an exchange process diagram for **Case 1**, in which the customer orders a specific puppy. The diagram must include commitments and contracts. Relate the diagram to REA by applying appropriate stereotypes.

### B. Exchange Process for a Puppy Type

Construct an exchange process diagram for **Case 2**, in which the customer specifies only the desired breed and gender. The diagram must include commitments and contracts. Relate the diagram to REA by applying appropriate stereotypes.

### C. Conversion Process

No conversion processes are explicitly described in the scenario. Identify at least one relevant conversion process and add it to your solution.
