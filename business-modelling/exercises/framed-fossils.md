# REA Exercise: Framed Fossils

## Exercise 1 — To Be Discussed During the Lesson

The business of a company is described below. Based on this description, complete the following tasks.

### Tasks

1. Construct a conceptual schema for the business using **REA**.

2. Assume that the company no longer delivers the pictures using its own personnel and cars but instead outsources this activity to a distributor. Explain how the schema constructed in Task 1 would change.

## Business Description

A company manufactures and sells framed fossils. The company consists of several subunits that autonomously manage procurement and sales. Some activities, however, are managed centrally, as described below.

The subunits procure fossils and frames from their suppliers. No individual supplier can provide both fossils and frames. The fossils to be framed are typically very expensive and are procured individually, often following a complex negotiation. Frames, in contrast, are relatively inexpensive and are ordered in large quantities to keep prices down.

Qualified personnel mount the fossils in the frames. When a picture has been completed, an inspector examines it to ensure that it meets the highest standards before it is shipped to a customer.

Customers usually buy only one framed fossil at a time, although a customer may sometimes order several pictures simultaneously. In most cases, the customer specifies the kind of fossil desired, for example, a *Tyrannosaurus rex* tooth. Sometimes, however, a customer orders a particular fossil.

Most pictures are expensive and fragile. They are therefore delivered directly to customers by the company’s own personnel, who use company cars for this purpose.

The company employs central personnel to mount and deliver the pictures. It also purchases the cars used for deliveries centrally.

## Information Requirements

The conceptual schema should support answers to the following questions:

1. Which customers have bought a picture costing more than €1,000?
2. Which customers have bought a picture but have not yet paid for it?
3. Which frame is the most popular for fossils costing more than €10,000?
4. Which suppliers can deliver amber fossils?
5. Which employees have delivered a picture to the customer Anders Andersson?
