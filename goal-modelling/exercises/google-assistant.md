# Goal-Modelling Exercise: Google Assistant

## Background

Google offers a service called **Google Assistant**. This personal assistant can help users pose search queries, play music, turn on lights at home, start various types of electronic equipment, and much more. Google Assistant is intended to be available on several different devices. One of these is **Google Home**, which combines a microphone and a speaker. Using Google Home, users can access Google Assistant by voice without holding a device.

## Exercise

Construct a **goal model for Google Assistant**.

### Minimum Requirements

The model must include at least:

* **6 goals**
* **6 means**
* **One means for every leaf goal**
* **One objective for every leaf goal**
* **6 influencers**
* **One influencer that is both a strength and a weakness**; mark this clearly in the diagram
* **One influencer that is both a threat and an opportunity**; mark this clearly in the diagram

### Items to Include

Your goal model must include all of the following items:

1. To make Google Assistant enjoyable to use
2. Apple offers Siri, a competitor to Google Assistant
3. To make people use Google Assistant several times a day
4. Amazon offers Echo, a competitor to Google Assistant
5. To ensure that Google Assistant can interact with many third-party apps and services
6. To collect a lot of data about users of Google Assistant
7. To make Google Assistant available on many devices
8. To show ads that are relevant to the user
9. To give answers to queries that are relevant to the user
10. To have a good microphone in Google Home
11. To cooperate with vendors that offer apps and services
12. People nowadays regularly use many apps and services on their smartphones

For each item, choose whether to represent it as a **goal**, **objective**, **means**, or **influencer**. You may need to reformulate some items so that they clearly express the type of model element you have chosen.

### Assumptions

You will need to make your own assumptions about Google Assistant. **State all such assumptions explicitly.**
